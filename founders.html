Our story

From self-taught to a shared studio

2020

Two paths into web development

Prothes and Shreyasi begin learning and building websites on their own — HTML, CSS and JavaScript first, using free resources like FreeCodeCamp and W3Schools.

2021–2023

From websites to full-stack

Shreyasi moves into full-stack development and WordPress; Prothes grows into WordPress, databases and Android development.

2022

Prothes completes B.Sc in CSE

Software engineering becomes his full-time focus, including Android apps and AI projects. Shreyasi continues her B.Sc in CSE at BSMRSTU.

2023–2024

First products go live

Setu POS, Krishi Bazar and Notun Khata ship for small businesses in and around Barisal.

2025–2026

PSBM Labs is formed

The two practices combine into one studio, with Angkan Barai joining as primary maintainer.

Life & work

Why we build as a couple

We learned to code the hard way — late nights, free tutorials and a lot of broken layouts. Along the way we realised the best products come from two people who trust each other enough to say “this isn’t good enough yet.”

That trust is how PSBM Labs runs today: shared responsibility for every release, honest status updates, and support that doesn’t end at launch. Angkan Barai, Prothes’s younger brother, keeps our shipped products stable after release.

Working from Barisal, we want to show that world-class apps and software can be built by a small, dedicated team in Bangladesh.

FAQ

About the founders

Who founded PSBM Labs?

PSBM Labs was founded by Prothes Barai and Shreyasi Madhu, a husband-and-wife team of software engineers based in Barisal, Bangladesh. Angkan Barai is co-founder and primary maintainer.

Why does a married couple running a studio help clients?

Every build gets two independent technical perspectives, shared accountability and no account managers in between — the engineers who scope your project are the ones who build and support it.

What does each founder focus on?

Prothes leads app and software development (Flutter, Kotlin, Firebase, databases, WordPress). Shreyasi focuses on full-stack web, WordPress, Django and SEO.

Where is the studio based?

Barisal, Bangladesh. We work with clients across Bangladesh and internationally, mostly over email and video calls.
