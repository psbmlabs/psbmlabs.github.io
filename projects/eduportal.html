EduPortal case study.

School management system covering attendance, results, and parent notifications for institutions, with a role-based portal for teachers and admins.

PSBM Labs · Live · Role: Full build & staff training

CategoryEducation · Web platform
Tech stackLaravel, jQuery, MySQL
StatusLive
Our roleFull build & staff training

The problem

A local educational institution was managing attendance and results on paper and in disconnected spreadsheets, which made term-end reporting slow and gave parents no visibility into their child's attendance or performance between report cards.

The solution

EduPortal is a role-based web platform where teachers mark attendance and enter results directly, admins get institution-wide reporting, and parents receive automatic notifications — replacing a paper-and-spreadsheet process with a single system of record.

Key features

  • Daily attendance marking with automatic absence notifications to parents
  • Result entry and term-report generation for teachers and admins
  • Role-based portal separating teacher, admin, and parent-facing views
  • Staff training and onboarding included as part of the rollout

The result

The institution now generates term reports in a fraction of the previous time, and parents get real-time attendance notifications instead of finding out about absences at the next report card. The platform is live and in daily use by staff.
