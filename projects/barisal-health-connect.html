HEALTHCARE · WEB PLATFORM

Barisal Health Connect case study.

Clinic appointment booking and patient record system for small healthcare providers, with SMS confirmations and a front-desk queue view.

PSBM Labs · In development · Role: Architecture & ongoing build

CategoryHealthcare · Web platform
Tech stackLaravel, MySQL, Bootstrap
StatusIn development
Our roleArchitecture & ongoing build

The problem

Small clinics were booking appointments over phone calls and a physical appointment book, which led to double-bookings, no-shows with no reminder system, and patient records scattered across paper files with no easy way to pull up history during a visit.

The solution

Barisal Health Connect gives small clinics a web-based booking and patient-record system: patients book a slot online or the front desk books on their behalf, an SMS confirmation goes out automatically, and doctors can pull up a patient's visit history in seconds instead of digging through paper files.

Key features

  • Online appointment booking with automatic SMS confirmation
  • Front-desk queue view showing today's bookings in order
  • Searchable patient records with visit history
  • Role-based access separating front-desk, doctor, and admin views

The result

The platform is in active development with a small healthcare provider as the first rollout partner; the architecture is built to extend to multiple clinic locations from a single admin account once the first deployment is validated.
