Before you hire anyone

8-point checklist for clients

  • Write the one problem the product must solve.
  • List who will use it and on which devices.
  • Decide the smallest version worth launching first.
  • Ask who owns the source code and design files.
  • Get scope, milestones and price in writing.
  • Confirm who handles Play Store / hosting accounts.
  • Ask what post-launch support is included and for how long.
  • Ask to see live products, not just screenshots.
Stay safe

Installing an APK safely

  • Download only from psbmlabs.github.io/apps or our Google Play page.
  • Check the app name and developer shown during install.
  • Allow “install unknown apps” only for the browser you used, then switch it off again.
  • Never share OTPs or passwords with any app that asks outside its login screen.
  • Beta builds can have bugs — report them by email and we fix them.
